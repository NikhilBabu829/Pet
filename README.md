🐾 [Pet]
Pet is a lively, interactive desktop companion application built exclusively for Windows and macOS. Inspired by classic screen pets but supercharged with modern features and productivity tools, this app brings your desktop to life with adorable, intelligent pixel-art pets.
Whether you need a companion to keep you company during long coding sessions, a quirky distraction, or a cute way to manage your tasks, Pet is designed to live right on your screen, interacting with your workflow in real-time.

✨ Core Features
👾 1. Pixel-Art Companions

Choose your new best friend from a diverse roster of beautifully animated pixel-style pets. Whether you want a classic cat or dog, or something a bit more extraterrestrial like an alien, there is a companion for everyone.

🏃 2. Lifelike Screen Simulation

Your desktop is their playground. Pets feature incredibly smooth animations as they run, walk, and explore your screen.
Playtime: They interact with digital on-screen toys.
Reactivity: They react dynamically to where your mouse is and what you are doing.
Attention Seeking: Ignore them for too long, and they might just make a cute, digital mess on your screen to force you to take a break and pay attention to them!

🪟 3. Window Manipulation (Mischief Mode)

These aren't just static overlays; they are aware of your environment. If left unattended for extended periods, your pets might get a little rebellious and start physically interacting with your application windows—pushing them left or right, panning them, or even resizing them to grab your attention.

💬 4. Dynamic Thought Bubbles

Ever wonder what your desktop pet is thinking? Random, context-aware thought bubbles will pop up over their heads, giving them distinct personalities and letting you know exactly what is on their mind.
⌨️ 5. Typing Mimicry & "Overheating"
Your pet works when you work! As you type, your pet will mimic your actions with their own little keyboard.
Bonus quirk: If you start typing furiously and your words-per-minute spikes, watch out—your pet will get "overheated" trying to keep up with your blazing speed!

🍅 6. Pet-Assisted Pomodoro Timer

Need to focus? Launch the built-in Pomodoro timer. Your pet will shift into productivity mode, helping you track your work and break intervals, ensuring you don't burn out (and neither do they).

📝 7. Visual "Memory" Notes

Ditch the physical sticky notes. You can give your pet a short note or reminder to "hold" for you. They will display this note floating right above their head as they wander around, ensuring you never forget your most important tasks.

🎨 8. Fully Customizable Skins

Keep things fresh by swapping out your pet's appearance. Unlock or select different skins to match your current aesthetic, mood, or desktop wallpaper.
